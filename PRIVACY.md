# Privacy

Snapfire collects nothing.

- It runs only when you click its button or press Alt+Shift+S, and only on that tab.
- To capture the whole page it scrolls through it once (so pictures that load on scroll appear), then scrolls back.
- The screenshot is made by Firefox on your computer and shown in a new tab. It is kept in memory for at most
  10 minutes, until that tab has picked it up. Nothing is uploaded; there is no server.
- No analytics, no tracking, no account, no ads.

Permissions: `activeTab` (the tab you clicked it in) and `scripting` (to measure and scroll the page). That's all.
